What We Do

Three services. One clear outcome.

Whether you need to understand where you stand, rebuild your documentation from scratch, or keep it current — there is a service that fits.

The Audit

A structured on-site review of your current compliance documentation. We identify gaps, assess risk, and deliver a clear written report with prioritised actions you can act on immediately.

  • On-site visit, half or full day
  • Review of existing compliance documentation
  • Gap analysis against Irish legislation
  • Written report with ranked priority actions
  • Follow-up call to walk through findings

The Overhaul

A complete rebuild of your SOP library — every procedure written to reflect how your operation actually runs, then branded and handed over ready to use. Risk assessments can be included as part of a full compliance package.

  • Full SOP suite across all departments
  • Risk assessments available as an addition
  • Branded with your identity and details
  • Staff sign-off registers included
  • Handover and team briefing session

The Partnership

Compliance is not a one-time task. As an ongoing partner we review your documents annually, update them when regulations change, and remain available whenever you need us.

  • Annual review of all documentation
  • Regulatory change updates
  • Quarterly check-in calls
  • Priority response for new procedures needed
  • Direct line to your consultant
